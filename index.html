01// MISSION_BRIEF

    

      

        

          
My work addresses a single, unified problem: how cyber risks emerge, propagate, and
          cascade through the interconnected infrastructure systems that societies depend on — and how
          AI, geospatial intelligence, and rigorous risk modeling can be combined to see those risks before
          they become failures.

          
Across more than a decade of research — from a Ph.D. in GIS-based risk modeling to graduate
          work in cybersecurity with an AI emphasis — I have built a coherent body of independent work:
          a published article on GIS-enabled cybersecurity and four completed platforms spanning geospatial
          risk intelligence, network security assessment, NIST-aligned risk scoring, and trustworthy
          document-grounded AI.

          
The objective ahead: resilience-informed decision-making for critical infrastructure
          protection — analysis that is transparent, evidence-grounded, and usable by the people who
          keep essential services running.
